# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

Flutter/Dart mobile app with a separately hosted Python/FastAPI backend.
Persistent storage provider is undecided.

## Users

People learning any topic, including learners preparing for exams.

## Product Purpose

Pricegent helps people move from collecting information to understanding it. It transforms user-provided study material into clear explanations, connected concepts, and practical learning activities.

## Positioning

Pricegent uses a structured knowledge-transformation pipeline to explain, connect, question, and apply supplied information rather than acting only as a prompt-and-response chatbot.

## Operating Context

Learners may provide notes, lecture material, articles, research papers, books or chapters, technical documentation, and other text or documents. The initial workflow is to create a study project, add or paste material, receive a structured explanation, and save it for later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first release is focused on project creation, text/document input, text extraction, concept and definition extraction, structured explanations, basic concept relationships, simple mind maps, and saving/retrieving processed knowledge.
- A complete study result should use the learner-defined S.E.E. principle: Sense the central idea, Exaggerate it through an explicitly imaginary model with its limits stated, then Energize the learner with a grounded application or exercise. Pair this with a visual concept map and active-recall questions.
- When a learner explicitly requests processing, their supplied material is sent to the configured Pricegent learning service; the service must ground concepts in exact source excerpts and clearly distinguish imagination from fact.
- More advanced autonomous research and multi-step workflows come later; external research and consequential actions require appropriate user permission.
- The intelligence backend must remain independent of the Flutter frontend and expose structured interfaces.
- The specific persistence provider and AI provider are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Pricegent and is a distinct product, not a continuation of Feedsalone. No logo, visual identity, or specific brand voice has been established.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied a product brief describing the knowledge-transformation pipeline, modules, quality requirements, and phased development roadmap. No customer research, testimonials, or performance claims were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Prefer accurate, traceable explanations over impressive but unsupported claims.
- Make complex information understandable without losing essential meaning.
- Use imagination to make mechanisms memorable while clearly naming where an analogy stops being accurate.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty.
- Help learners connect knowledge and apply it, not just summarize it.
- Keep users in control of external research and autonomous actions.
