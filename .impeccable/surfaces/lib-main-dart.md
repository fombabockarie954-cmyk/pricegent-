## Direction contract

THESIS: A familiar study folio turns supplied material into a guided, revisit-able learning path instead of another generic chat.

OWN-WORLD: Warm study-desk surfaces, blue-ink navigation, saffron index tabs, and restrained pencil rules; Material 3 controls remain familiar and touchable.

STORY: The learner adds their own notes, receives source-grounded concepts, then uses S.E.E.—Sense, Exaggerate, Energize—with an explicit imagination limit, a navigable concept map, and active recall to turn information into understanding.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand and profile sit above a large two-line learning promise. A compact, paper-toned “Start a new study” section places the material field and primary action next. “Pick up where you left off” follows with current study rows. A three-destination bottom navigation anchors Home, Studies, and Library. Signature move: a saffron folio tab carries each study's current learning state from the list into its detail view. The study guide stages facts, imagination with its boundary, application, connected concepts, and recall in a single scroll.
